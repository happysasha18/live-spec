### [node: director]

The first read of a person's message, and what holds it. The last two rows read *todo*: the idea shelf is specified and nothing in the tree keeps one, so its requirement carries the `[target]` marker and its rows name the landing rather than a test. The rows that read *built* are careful about what they claim. The graded scenarios are recorded runs against written expectations, so they hold the reading's shape rather than proving a live session went through the door; the row says so in its own words, and the replay row is what keeps a stale score from reading as a fresh one.
