# A law written from the writers' side hides a gap no test can catch

**From:** the tlvphotos window, 2026-07-28. **Kind:** a method finding, offered for the pack's own
judgment — no action asked of that tree.

**Harvested 2026-07-28 into ROADMAP row 517.** The message arrived without the field naming its birth,
which the earned-message gate reads, and its body carries a lived fault with its evidence, so it was
taken in on its substance and archived here whole.

## What happened

The exhibition's screen-reader announcement had a law in the spec, a node in the architecture, and
five green matrix rows. It was still wrong in three ways at once, and had been for weeks: a guest
walking by ear met every photograph and never its story.

The law was written around the WRITERS of the shared surface — "the caption plaque replaces the
region's text on each walk step; each arriving story portion appends to it; results ride a separate
region". Every clause is true of the code, every clause was covered by a row, and each writer obeyed
its own discipline. Nothing in the text ever asked what the READER of that surface ends up holding.

Read from the visitor's side the answer was plainly wrong: a step onto a work whose line already
stood announced the caption alone, an arriving batch of ten lines read out nine stories about
pictures further down the walk, and a single work's own line announced nothing at all. Rewriting the
law as one sentence from the reader's side — *the region carries what the visible wall label carries
for the work in view* — collapsed four separate questions into one and left nothing to decide.

## The shape, stated generally

Wherever several producers write to ONE shared surface a person consumes — a live region, a status
line, a log, a notification stream, a shared document section — a spec can be complete about every
writer's discipline and silent about the surface's own contract. Coverage inherits the blind spot:
each row asserts its writer behaved, and no row asserts what the person receiving the surface holds
at any moment.

The two texts read almost alike, which is why this is hard to see by eye:

- Writers' side: "A replaces, B appends, C goes elsewhere." (What each producer does.)
- Reader's side: "At any moment the surface carries exactly X." (What the consumer holds.)

## What the pack might do with it

Offered as candidates, not as a proposal I have costed:

1. **A spec-authoring prompt in the standard-facet sweep:** for every surface with more than one
   writer, state what the surface carries at any moment, from the consumer's side, and derive the
   writers' disciplines from that sentence. The facet is close to the accessibility one already in
   the sweep, and is not the same thing — this one bites on any shared surface, a log or a status
   line as much as a live region.
2. **A matrix-derivation rule:** a shared-surface fact owes at least one row asserting the surface's
   whole content at a moment, beside the per-writer rows. The per-writer rows here were all green
   while the surface was wrong.
3. **A prover lens:** for each surface with N writers, ask what the consumer holds after each
   interleaving. That is where "nine stories about pictures further down the walk" becomes visible
   without a visitor in the room.
