These two are policy about how the project works, so they wait for his word. Neither is touched
today.

is the engine behind the 31 gates. It converts every observation into permanent machinery, and it
carries no counterweight. My recommendation: replace its text with the standing rule the plan's
day 14 already puts to him, that a new check appears only in place of an old one. He decides,

carries his own standing instruction that a recurring problem gets an owner. It also feeds the
same regrowth. My recommendation: keep the instruction and drop the requirement that the owner be
machinery, so a recurring problem may be answered by a fix instead. He decides, because the
instruction is his.
