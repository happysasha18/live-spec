## Requirements — one line each, quoted, with who it reaches

1. **The channel law itself.** "exactly two channels carry everything between two agents: the receiver's inbox for a one-shot request to change something, and a published contract for a recurring read." (`SKILL.md:498-500`) — reaches every agent window in the pack; mechanically read by `guardrails/route_agent_transport.py`.

2. **A message must name real blocked work.** "A message names the sender's own blocked work, in the message... A message that cannot name such work is never sent." (`SKILL.md:506-508`) — reaches the sending agent depositing into another agent's inbox; enforced by `guardrails/check-earned-message.py`, run at the intake sweep.

3. **A referral goes back to the asker, not sideways.** "A referral travels back to whoever asked... Forwarding a neighbour's question to the owner of its zone is the defect this law names." (`SKILL.md:518-521`) — reaches the agent answering a forwarded question; enforced by `guardrails/check-wrong-referral.py`.

4. **Data never rides a message; a contract grants explicitly.** "Data never travels as a message, and a contract publishes nothing by default... Every field in that artifact leaves the producer's tree on the owner's explicit permission, recorded with its date and its author." (`SKILL.md:528-530`) — reaches the agent publishing a contract (the data producer) and the agent consuming it; the read-grant machinery in `scripts/read-grant.py` and `scripts/read-grant-ask.md`.

5. **An agent recognises a neighbour's zone on its own.** "An agent may meet a fault or a lack in something another agent's zone owns... It then scans for cards, finds the owner, and takes the channel that fits. An agent that waits to be told has made the owner its router." (`SKILL.md:533-537`) — reaches any agent that meets a fault in a zone it does not own; the neighbouring zone's own `.live-spec/agent.md` card, read via `guardrails/check-agent-card.py`.

6. **Two crossings, then the third goes to the owner.** "One question crosses between two agents twice, and the third crossing goes to the owner... The third crossing is named in the sender's own status report as a zone question the two could not settle." (`SKILL.md:538-541`) — reaches the two agents in a refer-and-re-send loop, and the human (the status report that names the unsettled question); shares its bound with the human-decision withdrawal loop.

7. **An unowned concern goes to the pack, work never stalls.** "A concern no agent's zone owns goes to the pack, and the work never stalls on ownership... It goes to the pack's inbox, and the pack answers who owns it." (`SKILL.md:543-546`) — reaches the pack's own inbox and the pack repo's assigned session, which must answer ownership.

8. **A neighbour's capability is taken through a channel, never copied locally.** "A capability another agent's zone owns is taken through one of the two channels. Building a local copy of a neighbour's capability is the violation the cards exist to prevent." (`SKILL.md:551-552`) — reaches any agent tempted to duplicate a capability instead of calling through the inbox or the published contract; the owning agent's ratification of any agent-initiated message.
